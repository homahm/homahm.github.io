## Short Bio

I am a Research Assistant at the UCLA Communication Department. I graduated with a Master of Science in Computer Science from the University of Southern California (USC). My work focuses on the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, and computational social science. My research experience includes building large-scale data pipelines and deploying multimodal AI models (LLMs, BERTopic) to analyze digital discourse and health misinformation on platforms such as TikTok and YouTube.
